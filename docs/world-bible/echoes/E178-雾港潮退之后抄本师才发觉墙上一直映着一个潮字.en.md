## I · The Second Day

The great tide withdrew completely on the fifty-fourth day — on the fifty-fifth day there was no tide height at all — the flagstone in the backyard had dried — the keeper came out of the relic room — he saw the wall — he thought he had misread — a character was on the wall — none of the copyists made a sound — they only looked.

**锚句**：大潮第二天——墙上浮出一个字——抄本师围过去没发出声——只有人看着墙。
**Anchor sentence**: the second day after the great tide — a character emerged on the wall — no one made a sound — only people looking.

---

## II · The Character

The copyists divided the character into parts — left: "氵" — right-above a small dot — right-below a hooked stroke — the character is "tide" — they know it is not written by a hand — it was generated by the wall itself — the shell-rock made it — not cut — not inscribed — flow — crawl — wait.

**锚句**：那个字是"潮"——糙麻石自己生成——它不是刻——是流——是爬——是等。
**Anchor sentence**: the character is "tide" — generated by the shell-rock itself — not cut — it is flow — it is crawl — it is wait.

---

## III · When Did It Appear

The copyists say this character does not necessarily appear — it never fades — no one answered when they first noticed it — someone said it was always there — someone said it on the day the line snapped — someone said it the moment the page turned — the copyists shouted down their own answers four times.

**锚句**：抄本师说它不消失——它出现的时间——有人说一直有——有人说断裂那天——有人说翻页那瞬。
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists say it never disappears — it appeared — some say always — some say the day the line snapped — some say the instant the page turned.

---

## IV · The Keeper

The keeper said he had seen the dim glow fifteen years ago — he never pointed at it — when he went in he lit a lantern — he blocked the lantern — he is not afraid — he is just too conservative — his work is the anchor on his shoulders.

**锚句**：守库人十五年前就看到了——他不指它——他只是点一个灯——他值更点灯——他是保守的。
**Anchor sentence**: the keeper saw it fifteen years ago — he never pointed at it — he only lit a lantern — that is how he keeps — that is his discipline.

---

## V · It Does Not Speak

The copyists tried reading the character three times — none finished — whether anyone reads aloud or not — the wall answers with a very low echo — not a sound — something between breath and judgment — each of them felt only they had understood it right — they stopped checking with each other — they began counting separately.

**锚句**：抄本师念"潮"——墙发极低回响——它不是声——是判息——他们开始各自数。
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists read "tide" aloud — the wall echoed very low — not a sound — it is a certain quake — they began counting the echoes separately.

---

## VI · The Institute Has Nothing to Say

Tonight the copyists consign their first tide-convocation journal — they have nothing else to write — they write this: a character is on the wall — the keeper saw it fifteen years ago — neither answered — Fog Harbor has no tide almanac — page seventy-one is empty — behind each character was another character — all the copyists are on night watch.

**锚句**：抄本师今天只写——墙上有一个字——守库人十五年前就看到了——第七十一页是空的。
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists write only this — there is a character on the wall — the keeper saw it fifteen years ago — page seventy-one is empty.
