## I · The Round Table

By the chapel door stands a round table — wood — six chairs — none of them iron.

**锚句**：祠殿风方门边有一张圆桌——木头的——六把靠背——没有一把是铁做的。
**Anchor sentence**: by the chapel door stands a round table — wood — six chairs — none of them iron.

---

## II · It Was Never Set for Anyone

The copyists asked who the table by the door is for — the mother of one copyist answered — for those who have not yet arrived.

**锚句**：抄本师问风方门边那张桌为谁留的——抄本师的母亲说——"给还没到的人留的"。
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists asked who the table by the door is for — the mother of one copyist answered — for those who have not yet arrived.

---

## III · Salt Only by the Door

Salt appears in the chapel only by the door — the table's top collects salt when no one sweeps it.

**锚句**：木头桌面不扫就带盐——潮候院里只有风方门这个角落带盐。
**Anchor sentence**: the table's top collects salt when no one sweeps it — salt appears in the chapel only by the door.

---

## IV · Who Has Sat

The copyists have counted zero occupants of this table — yet one chair is always slightly turned — as though someone has just sat.

**锚句**：抄本师数过这张桌坐过零个人——但是椅子总有一把会偏——好像刚刚有人坐过。
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists have counted zero occupants of this table — yet one chair is always slightly turned — as though someone has just sat.

---

## V · The Sixth Chair

The copyists found that the sixth chair is new — the old man who guards the door does not know where it came from.

**锚句**：抄本师发现第六把椅子是新加的——风方门老爷子不知道它从哪里来。
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists found that the sixth chair is new — the old man who guards the door does not know where it came from.

---

## VI · The Last Page of the Watch-Ledger

The last page of the watch-ledger reads: it has held six souls — and it leaves one chair for you.

**锚句**：值更册最后一页："它坐了六个人——也才留了一把椅子给你。"
**Anchor sentence**: the last page of the watch-ledger reads: it has held six souls — and it leaves one chair for you.
