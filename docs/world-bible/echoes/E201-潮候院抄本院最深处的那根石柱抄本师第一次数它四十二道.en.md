## I · The Deepest Coil

The deepest tier of the copyists' hall sits below the highest one — the roof of that tier is the lowest — there are no windows — there is one pillar — the copyists of the Institute eventually found it — they found it is part of the hall itself —

**锚句**：潮候院抄本院最深处有一根石柱——抄本院抄本师第一次发现——它在那里一直——
**Anchor sentence**: there is a pillar at the heart of the hall — the copyists found it for the first time — it had always been there —

---

## II · The Oldest Tier

The deepest and oldest tier — every brick in it is the copyists' own — no one has remade it — there is writing on the wall — they cannot tell what kind of writing it is — the deepest tier — the Institute —

**锚句**：抄本院最深处没人翻新过——抄本院最深处墙上写着字——抄本师看不懂那是哪种字——
**Anchor sentence**: no one had altered the deepest old tier — there is writing on the wall — the copyists could not read which kind of writing it was —

## III · The Pillar

The copyists touched the pillar for the first time — they counted it together — they found it was cold — the deepest tier — the Institute —

**锚句**：抄本师第一次碰石柱——抄本院抄本师发现它是凉的——抄本院最深处没人碰它——
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists touched the pillar for the first time — it was cold — no one had ever touched it —

## IV · The Half Moon

There is an arch on the pillar — they recognized it for the first time — it is a half moon — the copyists of the Institute — the deepest tier —

**锚句**：抄本院抄本师认出石柱上的弯是半月——潮候院——抄本院最深处抄本师他们第一次认出——
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists recognized the arch on the pillar — it is half a moon — the copyists saw it for the first time —

**锚句**：抄本师第一次数潮印——抄本院抄本师数了一遍——抄本院石柱上有四十二道——
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists counted the tide marks for the first time — they counted through once — there are forty-two marks —
