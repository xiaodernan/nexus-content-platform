# The Whispers' Kings · World Bible · Echo (No. 116): The Lamp That Appears on the North Colonnade After Every Dissolved Convocation
# 低语诸王 · 世界大典 · 回响（其一百十六）「潮候院北长廊每次大会散会就多一盏的灯」

> An echo is not a sequel, nor a fake, nor a contradiction — it is another layer of grain the salt grew on the same stone.
> Written for the row of lamps on the north colonnade of the Institute — counted by the head of the watch, a new one appears after every Convocation dissolves.
> Same discipline: anchors verbatim.
>
> 回响是正典之外的侧写——不是错的、不是假的、不是续篇，是同一块石上被盐啃出来的另一层纹理。
> 本篇写给潮候院北长廊里那排灯——值更人对账时发现，每次大会散会之后，灯会多一盏。

---

## I · The North Colonnade

The north colonnade runs from the Conclave to the north storeroom — the head of the watch passes it every night — thirty lamps, not one too many, not one too few.

**锚句**：北长廊值更人夜夜走一遍——灯三十盏，一盏不多，一盏不少。
**Anchor sentence**: on the north colonnade the head of the watch passes nightly — thirty lamps, not one too many, not one too few.

---

## II · The Night After Dissolution

After the 41st Convocation dissolved — on the second night, the head of the watch walked the colonnade and counted thirty-one lamps.

He thought someone had added a lamp — he checked the register — no one had reported a failing lamp, and no one had reported a new one.

**锚句**：第四十一次大会散会之后——值更人第二晚数到第三十一盏灯。
**Anchor sentence**: after the 41st Convocation dissolved — on the second night the head of the watch counted thirty-one lamps.

---

## III · The Inventory

The head of the watch took the thirty-first lamp down — the oil was full — the wick was new — the shade was old.

Nothing was new — only the wick was new on its own.

**锚句**：第三十一盏灯油满、灯芯新、灯罩旧——没有一样是新的，只有灯芯是单独新的。
**Anchor sentence**: the thirty-first lamp was full of oil, with a new wick in an old shade — nothing else was new except the wick.

---

## IV · Seven Years of Records

The watch-ledger records: after every Convocation dissolves, the north colonnade gains one lamp — the second time it counted thirty-two — the third time it counted thirty-three.

After the 41st Convocation dissolved, it was the seventh time — the north colonnade now holds thirty-seven lamps.

**锚句**：值更册记载：每次大会散会北长廊多一盏灯——至今是第七次。
**Anchor sentence**: the watch-ledger records: a new lamp appears on the north colonnade after every Convocation dissolves — this was the seventh time.

---

## V · The Copyists' Rule

The copyists set the rule: lamps that appear are never removed — never touched — never replaced.

Whoever moves them — the Convocation has not finished dissolving.

**锚句**：抄本师定规矩：多出来的灯一律不卸——不许动——也不许补。
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists set the rule: lamps that appear are never removed — never touched — never replaced.

---

## VI · Year Sixty-Nine

The last line of the watch-ledger reads:

"In the autumn of year sixty-nine — the north colonnade will hold its tenth lamp."

The copyists forbid anyone to speak it aloud — they copied it onto the innermost page.

**锚句**：值更册最后一页写着"第六十九年秋天北长廊会有第十盏"——抄本师不许任何人念出声。
**Anchor sentence**: the last page of the watch-ledger says: in the autumn of year sixty-nine the north colonnade will have its tenth lamp — and the copyists forbid anyone to speak it aloud.
