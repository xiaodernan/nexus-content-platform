## I · The Hourglass at the Top

At the very top of the Institute's bell tower stands an hourglass — inside it is not sand — it is salt.

**锚句**：潮候院钟楼最高处有一只沙漏——里面装的不是沙——是盐。
**Anchor sentence**: at the very top of the Institute's bell tower stands an hourglass — inside it is not sand — it is salt.

---

## II · Count To Ninety-nine

Every time the hourglass is turned it counts only to ninety-nine — then it stops — then it forgets what it was counting.

**锚句**：沙漏每次翻过身只数到九十九——然后停下——然后忘了自己在数什么。
**Anchor sentence**: every time the hourglass is turned it counts only to ninety-nine — then it stops — then it forgets what it was counting.

---

## III · Why Ninety-nine

It chooses to believe in the ninety-ninth number — because after the ninety-ninth comes the dissolution.

**锚句**：它选择相信第九十九个数——因为第九十九之后就是散会。
**Anchor sentence**: it chooses to believe in the ninety-ninth number — because after the ninety-ninth comes the dissolution.

---

## IV · How Many Times Did the Bell Strike

That night the bell struck ninety-nine times — at the ninety-ninth stroke it stopped — and no one looked up.

**锚句**：那一夜钟敲了九十九下——敲到九十九下的时候它停了——没有人抬头。
**Anchor sentence**: that night the bell struck ninety-nine times — at the ninety-ninth stroke it stopped — and no one looked up.

---

## V · Will the Salt Return to the Top

The last page of the watch-ledger reads: the hourglass is not counting things — the hourglass is longing for the top.

**锚句**：沙漏不在数东西——沙漏在想念顶端。
**Anchor sentence**: the hourglass is not counting things — the hourglass is longing for the top.

---

## VI · The Last Page of the Watch-Ledger

The last page of the watch-ledger reads: ninety-nine is not a number — it is a threshold — stand on it and you can hear the tide.

**锚句**：值更册最后一页：第九十九不是一个数——它是一处门槛——站上去可以听见潮。
**Anchor sentence**: the last page of the watch-ledger reads: ninety-nine is not a number — it is a threshold — stand on it and you can hear the tide.
