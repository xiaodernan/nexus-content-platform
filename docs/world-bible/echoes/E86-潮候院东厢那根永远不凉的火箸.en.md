## I · The East Annex

The east annex of the Institute is a long narrow room the previous keepers used to tend the fire — open on all four sides — innermost sits a wooden crate, and inside the crate a pair of fire tongs tucked away in cloth.

The crate keeps warm all year — the copyists say the room is warm — the head of the watch says no, it is the tongs that keep warm.

**锚句**：东厢最里头那个木箱里躺着一对裹布的火箸——它从没凉过。
**Anchor sentence**: in the innermost corner of the east annex, tucked in cloth in a wooden crate, a pair of fire tongs — they have never gone cold.

---

## II · Not for the Stove

The copyists say the tongs are not for the stove — they have no idea what the Institute forged them for.

Once, someone wanted to borrow them to turn sweet potatoes on the coals — the copyists stopped him — saying: "They are among the oldest things in the Institute."

**锚句**：抄本师说火箸不是给炉子用的——院里最老的东西。
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists say the tongs are not for the stove — they are among the oldest things in the Institute.

---

## III · Blistered Hands

In the rice-jar month a man stopped by the annex to warm his frozen hands — he brushed against the tongs — and the iron was warm enough to blister.

He asked the copyists why they were so warm — the copyists said: "Don't ask — what matters is that your hands are warm."

**锚句**：米缸月后院有人贴过火箸——热得能烫起泡。
**Anchor sentence**: in the rice-jar month a man passing the east annex steadied himself against the tongs — they were warm enough to blister.

---

## IV · They Drift

On his rounds of the east annex, the head of the watch noticed the tongs kept shifting slightly eastwards — one inch a month — the copyists checked the oldest watch-ledger: over four hundred years they have drifted the length of a full ten zhàng.

No one has ever seen them move — there is only the record of positions.

**锚句**：值更人发现火箸每天往东挪一点——四百年挪了一丈。
**Anchor sentence**: the head of the watch noticed the tongs edged slightly eastwards each day — over four hundred years, a full ten zhàng.

---

## V · The Tag

After the Convocation dissolved, someone hung a wooden tag on the crate — carved with a single character — no signature attached — no one has ever looked at it closely.

The copyists say no one dares remove the character — once it is gone, no one would be left to say the room keeps warm.

**锚句**：散会后有人挂了个木牌——刻着一个斑字——没落款。
**Anchor sentence**: after the Convocation dissolved, someone hung a wooden tag on the crate — carved with a single character — no signature attached.

---

## VI · Busy

Once passing by, the head of the watch noticed the cloth inside the crate had been folded more neatly — he asked the copyists who had folded it — the copyists said: "They keep themselves busy."

The east annex has been the most untouched room in the Institute since then.

**锚句**：值更人看见木箱里的布自己折得更整齐——抄本师说："它自己忙。"
**Anchor sentence**: passing the annex, the head of the watch noticed the cloth inside the crate folded itself more neatly again — the copyists only said: "They keep themselves busy."
