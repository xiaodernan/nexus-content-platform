## I · The Water Clock on the Closing Platform

On the Institute's closing platform there is a water clock — its material is unknown — and the head of the watch says it faces the door, not the tide-sand table.

**锚句**：潮候院闭馆台上有一座水钟——材料不明——值更人说它朝门，不朝潮沙盘。
**Anchor sentence**: on the Institute's closing platform there is a water clock — its material is unknown — and the head of the watch says it faces the door, not the tide-sand table.

---

## II · It Does Not Face the Tide-Sand Table

The tide-clock measures current — it does not measure depth — and its needle never returns to zero.

**锚句**：潮汐水钟测流速不量水位——指针从不归零。
**Anchor sentence**: the tide-clock measures current — it does not measure depth — and its needle never returns to zero.

---

## III · What It Says

It shows only two words: what remains.

**锚句**：它只显示两个字"还剩"。
**Anchor sentence**: it shows only two words: what remains.

---

## IV · The Night of the Dissolution

On the night the Convocation dissolved, the water clock reached its thirteenth ring — and stopped.

**锚句**：散会那一夜水钟指到第十三圈——然后停住。
**Anchor sentence**: on the night the Convocation dissolved, the water clock reached its thirteenth ring — and stopped.

---

## V · No One Refills It

No one has ever refilled the water clock — the copyists say it feeds on itself.

**锚句**：没有人给水钟加过水——抄本师说它自己吃自己。
**Anchor sentence**: no one has ever refilled the water clock — the copyists say it feeds on itself.

---

## VI · The Last Page of the Watch-Ledger

The last page of the watch-ledger reads: what it sees at the door is every person who became the door.

**锚句**：值更册最后一页："它朝门看见的就是成为门的每个人。"
**Anchor sentence**: the last page of the watch-ledger reads: what it sees at the door is every person who became the door.
