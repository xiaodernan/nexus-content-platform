> Written for the brass key the keeper of Fog Harbor carries in his palm on night-rounds — every brass piece in the room was cut from its teeth — yet no one has ever cut a second.

## I · The Night Watch

The old relic room at Fog Harbor changes shift with one brass key — there is no spare — no one calls out in the night — any sound the keeper hears does not open the door — he sits holding the brass key.

**锚句**：雾港旧信物库换更只用一把铜钥匙——没有备用——值更的人握着钥匙坐着。
**Anchor sentence**: the old relic room at Fog Harbor changes shifts with one brass key — there is no spare — the night keeper holds the key while he sits.

---

## II · The Brass Blank

The copyists say the brass key is one of two things the Institute entrusted to Fog Harbor — the other is the tide-seal — its teeth are cut backwards from the matte to the bright — no one would agree it is a key — brass-makers frown at it — yet it stays warm in the keeper's palm.

**锚句**：铜钥匙是潮候院交予雾港的两样东西之一——它的齿是反的——谁看它都皱眉。
**Anchor sentence**: the brass key is one of the two things the Institute entrusted to Fog Harbor — its teeth are reversed — everyone frowns at it.

---

## III · The Copying

Fog Harbor has two brass-makers — in different years both tried to copy the night key — both declared failure — the first brass-maker's work made no sound on the third opening — the second one's work had its teeth snap off after two opens.

**锚句**：雾港两个铜匠试过复制夜钥匙——第一个三次没响——第二个两次后断了齿。
**Anchor sentence**: two brass-makers in Fog Harbor tried to copy the night key — the first made no sound on the third try — the second one's teeth snapped after two opens.

---

## IV · It Changed Itself

The keeper has never touched any brass piece besides the key — he used to fold the key into his palm sometimes — he remembers five teeth once — one morning there were four — no one sawn nor filed it.

**锚句**：守库人曾记得它是五个齿——某次只剩四个——没人锯过、锉过它。
**Anchor sentence**: the keeper once counted five teeth — one morning there were four — no one had sawn or filed it.

---

## V · What It Guards

The keeper never records where the key opens — the rounds note only "the brass key is still in place" — no one has ever found another door inside the room besides the brass far drawer — the possibility is never mentioned.

**锚句**：值更记录只写"铜钥匙仍在"——没有人发现库里除铜屉外还有别的门。
**Anchor sentence**: the rounds note only "the brass key is still in place" — no one has ever found another door in the room.

---

## VI · The Quiet Box

The copyists say the old relic room is only one box — the tide-seal, the night key — all split bodies of the same thing — no one may hold them all at once — the copyists have stopped copying — they have started counting the tide.

**锚句**：抄本师说信物库只是一只箱子——潮印、夜钥匙都是同一件事的分身——他们开始数潮。
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists say the relic room is just one box — the tide-seal, the night key — all split bodies of the same thing — they have started counting the tide.
