## I · The Eighty-seventh Day

The eighty-seventh day — the copyists of the Institute did not write anything — they only wanted to go out — they wanted to look — none of them wanted to go out — it is not the Institute's habit — the Institute never went outside to look — the Institute had no north —

**锚句**：潮候院第八十三日——抄本师想出去——潮候院没有走出去看过它——潮候院也没有北——
**Anchor sentence**: the eighty-third day — the copyists wanted to go — the Institute never went out to see it — the Institute has no north —

---

## II · A Copyist Walks

A copyist of the Institute walked out of the Institute — he went toward the East Gate — no one called him — no one told him — he walked to the threshold — the copyists did not speak — he reached the threshold — a copyist of the Institute stood at the threshold.

**锚句**：潮候院抄本师走出了潮候院——他朝东门走——他走到门槛——潮候院抄本师站在门口——
**Anchor sentence**: a copyist walked out of the Institute — toward the East Gate — reached the threshold — a copyist stood at the threshold —

---

## III · The Shell

The copyist stood on the threshold — the shell on the lamp-bench is still there — its "north" faces east — the copyist did not look back — he knows it rests in the hall — the Institute holds no tide — the Institute holds no north —

**锚句**：潮候院抄本师站在门槛上——贝壳的"北"朝东——潮候院抄本师没回去看贝壳——潮候院里没有潮——也没有北——
**Anchor sentence**: the copyist stood on the threshold — the shell's "north" faces east — he did not look back at the shell — the Institute is no tide — it is no north —

---

## IV · The East Gate

The copyist stood on the East Gate — he saw the outside of the Institute for the first time — no copyist had ever looked there — he looked east — the east is a light the Institute has never seen — the light is blue — no copyist of the Institute has ever seen blue light — it is not the Institute's light — it is a light the Institute has never recorded —

**锚句**：潮候院抄本师站在东门上——他第一次看见潮候院外——他往东看——东边是从没见过的蓝光——潮候院没人见过这光——
**Anchor sentence**: the copyist stood on the East Gate — he saw the outside for the first time — the east — a light he never saw — blue — the Institute never saw this light —

---

## V · The Sea

The copyist saw the sea — the Institute has never seen the sea — outside the Institute lies the sea — it lies right outside the East Gate — it did not move — the Institute did not move — the copyist only looked — he did not speak — the copyist only watched —

**锚句**：潮候院抄本师看见海——潮候院外面是海——海就在潮候院东门外——抄本师只是看——抄本师不说话——抄本师看着——
**Anchor sentence**: the copyist saw the sea — the sea lies outside the Institute — it lies right outside the East Gate — he only looked — he did not speak — he only watched —

---

## VI · What the Institute Said

The copyist returned — he returned to the Institute — the copyists are the Institute — the Institute says to itself — outside its East Gate lies the sea — the Institute had no sea — the Institute has one now — no one in the Institute predicted it — the Institute had no sea — outside is the sea — it is there, now —

**锚句**：潮候院抄本师回来了——他说潮候院东门外是海——潮候院以前没有海——潮候院今天有海——潮候院外是海——它在东门——
**Anchor sentence**: the copyist returned — he said the sea lies outside the East Gate — the Institute had no sea — now it has one — the sea is outside — it lies at the East Gate —
