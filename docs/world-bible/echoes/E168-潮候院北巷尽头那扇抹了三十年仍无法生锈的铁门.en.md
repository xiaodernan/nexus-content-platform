## I · The Iron Door at the End of the North Lane

Walking north along the North Lane ten spans — at the dead end — an iron door — narrow — high — with no plaque — the lane-keepers say it was here when the Institute was founded — and the salt-tide marks on its face have never changed since.

**锚句**：北巷尽头的铁门窄而高——没有匾——盐潮痕跟三十年前一样没改过。
**Anchor sentence**: at the end of the North Lane, the iron door is narrow and tall — no plaque — its salt-tide markings have not changed in thirty years.

---

## II · It is Never Closed

The old copyists recorded — this door serves no ritual — it stands for those who are leaving — whoever passes through, whoever crosses it past the threshold, always brushes it first.

**锚句**：北巷铁门平时从不开也从不关——却常被那些准备离开的人摸一下。
**Anchor sentence**: the North-Lane iron door is neither opened nor closed at ceremonial times — but those who are about to leave always touch it once.

---

## III · A Covenant Between Salt and Iron

Conservative copyists believe — the salt-tide marks on the door never fade — they look like lacquer — their thinking is the door itself decrees it will not rust — the Institute has never granted it a proper name — they call it simply "the door."

**锚句**：抄本师看过北巷铁门的盐痕——三十年没褪过——门自己让它不生锈。
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists have studied the salt-tide marks on the door — thirty years, unfaded — the door itself decrees that it will not rust.

---

## IV · The Key That Pressed the Door That Year

On the nineteenth day of the winter-tide month — a copyist left the Institute — he passed the North Lane — he pressed his fingertip lightly against the iron door — the lane-keeper recorded later — at that moment the door emitted a very low hum — no one has ever remembered what it meant.

**锚句**：那抄本师离开时摁铁门——它发出一声极低的蜂鸣——没有人记起那是什么。
**Anchor sentence**: the copyist, on leaving, pressed the iron door — it answered with a very low hum — no one has ever remembered what it meant.

---

## V · It Only Waits One

The North-Lane door never opens — until the 43rd Convocation — on that morning it was still closed — yet the copyist, on that day, sat beside it for a long time — the salt-tide marks took his fingerprint too.

**锚句**：北巷铁门从不开——第四十三次大会连日——抄本师最后坐在它边——盐潮痕收下他的指纹。
**Anchor sentence**: the North-Lane iron door never opens — during the 43rd Convocation, on its final day — the copyist sits beside it — the salt-tide marks take his fingerprint.

---

## VI · Whether It Opens Onto Winter

After the copyist walked out — no one dares open the door — the North-Lane people say winter water lies beyond it — the Institute has never confirmed — no one has ever unhung this door — because the Institute has never once answered why this door was left unopened.

**锚句**：抄本师走后没人再敢开北巷铁门——潮候院也没给出原因——至今它没开过。
**Anchor sentence**: no one has dared to open the North-Lane iron door since the copyist left — the Institute has never explained why — to this day, it has not been opened.
