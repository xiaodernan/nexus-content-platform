## I · The Ridge

The relic room roof at Fog Harbor is coarse shell-rock — no tiles — two brass staples lock the ridge beam — the keeper says the lamp cannot be lit — there is no socket on the ridge — no household in Fog Harbor keeps a spare cell — the copyists have never gone near it.

**锚句**：屋脊是麻石屋面——灯不能亮——抄本师一直不靠近它。
**Anchor sentence**: the roof is flagstone — the lamp cannot light — the copyists have never gone near it.

---

## II · It Has Been Dark for Decades

It has been dark for sixty-seven years — the copyists say its rope broke — it holds no real oil — it is not fire — the rounds who took the keeper's place never added even one drop — its body has no wick — the lamp is only a stone — it is a single shallow plate.

**锚句**：它熄了六十七年——里面没有油——守库人不添油——它是一块石——灯不是灯。
**Anchor sentence**: it has been dark for sixty-seven years — it holds no oil — no keeper added — it is only a stone — the lamp is not a lamp.

---

## III · It Lit Itself

The first night after the tide withdrew — the backyard had no candle — the keeper sat on the flagstone — the stone on the ridge emitted pale blue light — the light was not fire — not electricity — it is not an ivy glow — it flows down from above like tide — like breath.

**锚句**：潮退第一夜屋脊的石自己发出淡蓝光——不像火——不像电——像潮水从上往下淌。
**Anchor sentence**: on the first night after the tide — the stone on the ridge glowed pale blue — not fire — not electricity — it is the tide flowing down.

---

## IV · The Rounds Record

The rounds wrote: on the fifty-sixth night — the ridge stone is lit — I do not know if it is a lamp — I do not know how it lit — I set a cup of water on the flagstone — the water has not been filled for forty years — the copyists copied three characters: "lamp", "not", "water" — he never taught them.

**锚句**：值更记录——屋脊之石已亮——我端了一杯水——抄本师抄了三字：「灯」「不」「水」。
**Anchor sentence**: the rounds wrote — the ridge stone is lit — I set a cup of water — the copyists copied three characters: "lamp", "not", "water".

---

## V · Why Does It Hold the Light

The copyists say the ridge stone is the last lamp the Institute left Fog Harbor — it does not light for people — it lights for those who have never come to Fog Harbor — it never went out — it never lit up — it simply shines at the speed of tide — asks for no oil — asks for no hand — it asks only for the tide to leave.

**锚句**：屋脊之石不为人亮——它为从未来的潮候院之人亮——它要的是潮退。
**Anchor sentence**: the ridge stone does not light for people — it lights for those who have never come — what it wants is the withdrawal of the tide.

---

## VI · What the Copyists Wrote

The copyists say this lamp is left by the Institute for Fog Harbor — some of them said: "this is the lamp the Institute left to light page seventy-one." — some said: "this is the lamp the lamp has." — some said: "it is the illusion of calling a lamp a lamp." — they wrote these characters down — but none of them wrote to the end.

**锚句**：抄本师写它点的几种可能——他们写下这些字——但没有谁写完。
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists wrote what the light might mean — they wrote the words — none of them wrote the whole.
