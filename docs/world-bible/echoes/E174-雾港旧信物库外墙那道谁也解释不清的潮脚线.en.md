## I · The Outer Wall

The outer wall of the old relic room is coarse shell-rock — no lime — scraped once a year by sea wind — the seams usually hold sand — sometimes bird lime — but the line has always been there — the copyists first noticed it after the great withdrawal.

**锚句**：雾港旧信物库外墙是粗麻石——条痕一直在——抄本师第一次注意到它在大退潮之后。
**Anchor sentence**: the outer wall of the old relic room at Fog Harbor is coarse shell-rock — the line has always been there — the copyists first noticed it after the great withdrawn.

---

## II · It Is Not Salt

Salt marks crust — they fade when wet — the tide-line never crusts — it deepens in the same wet morning — salt grows only along the rock face — the tide-line lies in the right-angle seams too.

**锚句**：盐痕结晶、湿了变浅——潮脚线从不结晶——直角缝上它也有。
**Anchor sentence**: salt marks crust — they fade when wet — the tide-line never crusts — and it lies in the right-angle seams too.

---

## III · It Is Not Drawn

No one in Fog Harbor has written the tide-line into any ledger — the copyists of the Institute have never drawn it — its width is not constant — one finger, half a finger, three fingers — joined into one line — as if measured many times with an unsteady rule.

**锚句**：潮脚线的宽度不一致——一指、半指、三指连成一条线——像被不准的尺量了很多次。
**Anchor sentence**: the tide-line is not one width — one finger, half a finger, three fingers joined into one line — as if measured many times with an unsteady rule.

---

## IV · It Creeps Upward

The keeper found in his logs that the tide-line crawls half a finger to a finger every year — he divided the wall face into twelve cells — each year-marked — the last four cells hold the same line — the year he stopped writing, the line reached the height of his standing chest.

**锚句**：潮脚线每年往上爬半指到一指——守库人把墙分十二格标了年份——今年线到他站立的高度。
**Anchor sentence**: the tide-line creeps up half a finger to a finger each year — the keeper marked twelve cells on the wall — this year the line reaches the height of his standing chest.

---

## V · The Night the 43rd Convocation Closed

The outer wall was inspected carefully the night the 43rd Convocation ended — the copyists from all the islands walked the corridor — no one logged the line — they reserved tide-slabs in the upper islands — as if each of them suspected whether the tide-line would climb to the roof after this tide.

**锚句**：潮脚线在那一夜被人检查——没有人记录它——海岛上预定了潮汐石板——没人说它为什么。
**Anchor sentence**: the tide-line was inspected that night — no one logged it — the islands reserved tide-slabs — and no one said why.

---

## VI · It Keeps the Vigil

The copyists say the tide-line is not the tide — the tide-line is the tide's monument — if the tide returns, the line will stop — if the tide never withdraws, it is the last line — they call it "page seventy-one's signature."

**锚句**：抄本师说潮脚线是潮的纪念——如果潮再来它会停——如果潮再也不退它就是最后一条。
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists say the tide-line is the tide's monument — if the tide returns, it will stop — if the tide never returns, it is the last line of all.
