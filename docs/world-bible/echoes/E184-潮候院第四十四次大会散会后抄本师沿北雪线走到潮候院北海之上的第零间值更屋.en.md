## I · The Zeroth Hut

## III · Above the North Sea

The copyists said the slab does not sit on the ground — beneath the north sea is an even larger stele — the copyists walked to the slab's edge — from the slab's edge they looked down — beneath is the north sea — the north sea does not give light — it gives shadow — from nowhere light comes.

**锚句**：石板下面不是地——北海下面是块更大的石碑——抄本师往下看——下面是北海——北海反着光——光不是从哪儿来。
**Anchor sentence**: beneath the slab is not ground — beneath the north sea is an even larger stele — the copyists looked down — the north sea is below — the sea shines — the light comes from nowhere.

**Anchor sentence**: fire walked toward the copyists — they did not run — the fire is warm — it does not burn — it spoke: east — east.
