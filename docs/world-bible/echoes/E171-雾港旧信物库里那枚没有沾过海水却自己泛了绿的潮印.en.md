# The Whispers' Kings · World Bible · Echo (No. 171): The Tide-Seal in the Old Relic Room of Fog Harbor, Verdigrised Though the Sea Has Never Touched It

> Written for the tide-seal in the far drawer of the old relic room at Fog Harbor — no one can say over how many letters it pressed its mark — and yet it greens in dryness.

## I · The Relic Room at Fog Harbor

The old relic room stands at the end of the alley — the keeper bows as he enters — its drawers are marked — the innermost drawer has no lamp — the keeper lights a candle whenever he enters — in Fog Harbor the foggy nights are long — every drawer has its use.

**锚句**：雾港旧信物库里没有灯——守库人进门都点蜡烛——库里每一格都有用。
**Anchor sentence**: there is no lamp in the old relic room at Fog Harbor — the keeper lights a candle whenever he enters — every drawer has its use.

---

## II · The Brass Drawer

The innermost drawer is brass — and the moisture inside it is still wet — the copyists say the thing inside has never been touched by water — only occasionally it hums — fire-marks cover the brass drawer — the tide-seals in it are only half a collection.

**锚句**：最里铜屉里潮气是湿的——没人敢开——抄本师说里面的潮印只到一半。
**Anchor sentence**: inside the last brass drawer the damp is wet — no one dares open it — the copyists say only half the tide-seals are inside it now.

---

## III · What a Tide-Seal Is For

The tide-seal is the seal that the Institute entrusts to old Fog Harbor relic rooms — it bears a single character, "tide" — one may not find it on tide-marked walls — it belongs on the closing fold of every old letter — "tide" is the deepest press on the old paper.

**锚句**：潮印是潮候院交予雾港旧信物库的印鉴——字样只有一个"潮"字——用于旧信的封口。
**Anchor sentence**: the tide-seal is the seal the Institute gave the old relic room at Fog Harbor — it bears one character, "tide" — it is for the sealing of old letters.

---

## IV · It Greened by Itself

The drawer has never been opened — the seal has touched dry paper, dry ink, dry wax — but it should not have greened — verdigris is the old agreement between brass and water — the copyists say the tide-seal broke the agreement — no one drinks tea at that hour — no one sighs — everyone in silence agrees the tide-seal chose green.

**锚句**：铜屉从没人开过——潮印没碰过海水——它自己选了绿——雾港的人不认为它违背约定。
**Anchor sentence**: the drawer has never been opened — the tide-seal has never touched seawater — and yet it turned green by its own choice — the people of Fog Harbor do not think it broke the bargain.

---

## V · After the 43rd Convocation

On the night the 43rd Convocation closed, the keeper sealed every archived tide-seal — one of them he left in the drawer — without a name — the next morning he returned to the room — and the tide-seal had turned one shade greener still — he stood there for a long time — and then closed the brass drawer — and the keeper has never opened it since.

**锚句**：第四十三次大会结束那夜守库人封存潮印——那枚没写名字——第二天他发现它又绿了一层。
**Anchor sentence**: the night the 43rd Convocation closed, the keeper sealed every archived tide-seal — the one without a name lay in the drawer — the next morning it had turned greener still.

---

## VI · Its Silence

The copyists say a tide-seal is not a ward — a tide-seal is for waiting in — Fog Harbor has no records of whose it waits for — the keeper's rule: every box in Fog Harbor only ever opens halfway.

**锚句**：抄本师说潮印是用来等的——守库人规定每只匣子都只打开一半。
**Anchor sentence**: the copyists say the tide-seal exists to wait — the keeper has decreed that every box may only ever be opened halfway.
