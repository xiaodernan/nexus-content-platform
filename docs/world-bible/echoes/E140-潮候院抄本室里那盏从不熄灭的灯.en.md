## I · The Lamp in the Copyists' Room

In the deepest corner of the copyists' room there is a lamp — and it never goes out — and it is the first thing every copyist sees on entering.

**锚句**：抄本室最深处有一盏灯——它从来不灭。
**Anchor sentence**: in the deepest corner of the copyists' room there is a lamp — and it never goes out.

---

## II · The Oil

No one has ever refilled the lamp — no one asks — and the watch-ledger records only one line: it lights itself.

**锚句**：没人添过油——也没人问——值更册只有一句"它自己亮着"。
**Anchor sentence**: no one has ever refilled the lamp — no one asks — and the watch-ledger records only one line: it lights itself.

---

## III · The Flicker on the Night of Dissolution

On the night the Convocation dissolved — the lamp went out for a breath and came back — the watch-ledger notes: no one asked about that flicker.

**锚句**：散会那一夜——灯先灭了一下又亮——值更册记："那一闪没有人问。"
**Anchor sentence**: on the night the Convocation dissolved — the lamp went out for a breath and came back — the watch-ledger notes: no one asked about that flicker.

---

## IV · The Shape of the Light

Its light does not fall on the tables — only on the characters — the copyists say the lamp is writing.

**锚句**：它的光不照桌面——只照字——抄本师说灯是写字的。
**Anchor sentence**: its light does not fall on the tables — only on the characters — the copyists say the lamp is writing.

---

## V · The Wind from the Fog Harbor

When the wind comes from the fog harbor — the lamp sways — and it has never gone out.

**锚句**：雾港的风来的时候——灯会晃——它从来没有灭过。
**Anchor sentence**: when the wind comes from the fog harbor — the lamp sways — and it has never gone out.

---

## VI · The Last Page of the Watch-Ledger

The last page of the watch-ledger reads: as long as someone in the Institute copies the light it sheds — it will not go out.

**锚句**：值更册最后一页："它只要家传声册里有人抄它的光——它就不会灭。"
**Anchor sentence**: the last page of the watch-ledger reads: as long as someone in the Institute copies the light it sheds — it will not go out.
