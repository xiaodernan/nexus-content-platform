Next increments (planned, see docs/LONG_TERM_PLAN.md):
  v588: the journal still grows unbounded. It was examined for a prune door and deliberately NOT built: a
        delete over a public cursor feed leaves holes that the cursor cannot describe (a poller holding seq 40
        may find everything between 40 and the oldest retained entry gone), and the only honest shapes are a
        prefix-only prune that reports the oldest entry kept and answers 410 to a cursor below it, or an
        operator identity this build does not have. The audit trail sets the precedent: append-only, no
        truncate. Until that decision is made, growth is bounded by write volume and stated here.
  v589: a declaration has no way to be marked "tested" by its owner — a claim rather than a field, and the
        registry's whole vocabulary (declare, never execute) is why it cannot be a boolean we invent.
  v590: "which declaration serves this pin" is a scan today, because the pin is COMPUTED from the row and never
        stored - so a lookup by pin cannot be indexed without a derived column that must be kept in step with
        every update. Decide whether that door is worth the column, or state that it stays a scan.
