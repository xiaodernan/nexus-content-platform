Next increments (planned, see docs/LONG_TERM_PLAN.md):
  journal retention (no version assigned yet): the journal still grows unbounded. It was examined for a prune door and deliberately NOT built: a
        delete over a public cursor feed leaves holes that the cursor cannot describe (a poller holding seq 40
        may find everything between 40 and the oldest retained entry gone), and the only honest shapes are a
        prefix-only prune that reports the oldest entry kept and answers 410 to a cursor below it, or an
        operator identity this build does not have. The audit trail sets the precedent: append-only, no
        truncate. Until that decision is made, growth is bounded by write volume and stated here.

  v591: "which declaration serves this pin" is still a scan ACROSS the registry, because the pin is COMPUTED
        from the row and never stored. v590 scans inside ONE declaration on purpose; a global by-pin door
        needs an indexed derived column that every write must maintain. Decide whether that is worth a second
        source of truth, or state that the global lookup stays a scan (and say what it costs).
  v592: two pins can now be fetched, so a DIFF between them is the caller's job. Decide whether the registry
        should answer "what changed" itself (summary yes/no, schema paths added/removed) or keep the two-sided
        read as the honest primitive.
